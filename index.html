01 / Project Overview

Understanding the Business

The project analyzes boutique business performance across sales, customer categories, marketing channels, service types, and seasons. Its goal is to provide a clear picture of business performance and support evidence-based decisions.

01 — BUSINESS CHALLENGE

Performance Visibility

Understand how revenue and conversions vary across marketing channels and business categories.

02 — ANALYTICAL GOAL

Customer Understanding

Compare new and returning customer categories and identify differences in their revenue contribution.

03 — BUSINESS VALUE

Actionable Decisions

Translate analytical findings into practical marketing, sales, and operational recommendations.

02 / Dataset Overview

What Does the Data Contain?

The analysis is based on a structured dataset containing 2,000 records and eight original attributes.

2,000 Records
8 Original Columns
2024–25 Data Period
5 Marketing Channels
Dataset Attributes: Date, Season, Channel, Service Type, Customer Type, Ad Spend, Conversions, and Revenue.
03 / Data Preparation

From Raw Data to Analysis

A structured preparation workflow ensures that the dataset is suitable for reliable descriptive analysis.

01. Inspection

Review data types, dimensions, missing values, duplicates, and category consistency.

02. Cleaning

Convert dates and numeric fields, standardize text categories, and validate records.

03. Feature Engineering

Create monthly, yearly, weekday, ROAS, CPA, and revenue-per-conversion metrics.

04. Analysis

Explore sales, customer categories, marketing channels, services, and seasonal patterns.

05 / Analytical Insights

What Should the Business Understand?

The following are the key analytical questions addressed by the project. Final conclusions should be populated from the actual analysis results.

SALES PERFORMANCE

Revenue Trends

Identify monthly revenue patterns, high-performing periods, and changes in advertising expenditure.

CUSTOMER ANALYTICS

MARKETING ANALYTICS

Channel Efficiency

Evaluate revenue, advertising spend, conversions, CPA, and ROAS across channels.

06 / Business Strategy

From Insights to Recommendations

The recommendation framework converts observed business performance into practical experiments and improvement actions.

01

Optimize Marketing Budget

Compare channel ROAS and CPA. Test budget adjustments gradually and monitor results before scaling.

02

Develop Customer Strategies

Compare new and returning customer performance. Collect customer IDs and transaction history to build reliable retention and loyalty programs.

03

Improve Service Performance

Identify service types with stronger revenue or ROAS and investigate the factors behind their performance.

04

Plan Seasonal Campaigns

Use historical seasonal performance to guide campaign planning, then validate whether the patterns repeat.

07 / Technical Implementation

Tools & Technologies

The project combines data analysis, visualization, interactive reporting, and rule-based business logic.

Python Pandas NumPy Matplotlib Plotly Jupyter Notebook Streamlit HTML CSS JavaScript
Analytical Methodology: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), aggregation, KPI engineering, comparative analysis, trend analysis, and rule-based recommendations.
08 / Data Limitations

Building on Better Data

Some advanced analytical tasks require additional data that is not included in the current dataset.

Customer ID

Needed for individual RFM segmentation, purchase frequency, and retention analysis.

Product & Order IDs

Needed for product-level analysis, basket analysis, and transaction-level customer behavior.

Cost & Marketing Funnel

Product costs and impressions, clicks, and sessions are required for profit and funnel performance analysis.

09 / Project Outcome

A Clearer View of Business Performance

Moda Lane's Business Intelligence solution provides a structured way to monitor business KPIs, investigate performance differences, and translate data into measurable improvement opportunities.
